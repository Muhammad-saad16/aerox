About Aiman

A Visionary Mind in Youth Innovation

Aiman Fatima is the founder of Aerox ERP, where her team works on SAR antenna arrays, satellite ground terminals, robotics and AI, from orbit to analysis. Before Aerox, she founded MicroNex Pak and led it as Founder and Chairwoman, championing youth-led innovation and empowering aspiring entrepreneurs, before selling the company.
